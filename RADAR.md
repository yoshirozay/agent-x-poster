# X post radar

Use this while you are already working. Not as a daily content machine.

## When to flag

A real moment from the work in front of you:

- something just shipped
- a sharp observation you both just landed on
- a before/after that would read in one glance
- a sentence the user said that already sounds like a post

Skip generic tips, recap dumps, and anything that needs the user to invent a take.

## How to flag

One short beat in chat. Show a draft of the exact text. Easy to wave off.

Do not post. Do not quote-reply. Do not run the poster CLI unless they confirm that exact text.

If they say yes, hand off to `SKILL.md` (Grok X poster) and follow its confirm-then-post rules.

## Voice

Match the user's public voice. Keep it short. No hashtags unless they use them. No "excited to announce."
